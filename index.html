About

Learn More Who We Are

Your career, our network, one shared goal

SIT Consulting Services is a Pune-based IT placement consultancy built for professionals with 0–5 or more years of experience who want more than a job portal listing. We work directly with candidates: rebuilding profiles, opening doors through our HR network, and staying involved through every interview round until an offer is signed. Our approach is hands-on by design: instead of mass-applying on your behalf, we match you to verified, currently-open roles, prepare you specifically for each opportunity, and keep pushing until the result is a placement, not just a callback.

Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to Common Questions

Is it really pay-after-placement?

The majority of our fee is due only after you've been successfully placed through our support. A separate, smaller registration fee is charged at sign-up to bring you into our active pipeline and begin the process.

What experience levels do you work with?

We support IT professionals with 0 to 5 years of experience.

How long does the placement process take?

Timelines vary by candidate, role, and market conditions, but our team stays actively engaged with you, from registration through profile building, matching, and interview prep, until you're placed.

What if I don't get placed?

We continue working with you as part of your registration, sharing new opportunities, refining your profile, and preparing you for further interviews.

Do you charge for interview preparation or profile optimization separately?

No. These are included as part of our core service once you've registered.

How many mock interviews will I get?

After registration, we take your first mock interview to identify where your skillset is lacking, then provide resources to help you improve. Once you've upgraded your skills and technologies, we schedule another mock interview, and repeat this process as many times as needed.
